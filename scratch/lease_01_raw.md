## RESIDENTIAL RENTAL AGREEMENT

Agreement 01 - Standard residential tenancy

TEST DATA NOTICE: This is a fictional synthetic agreement created solely for testing ClauseLens. It is not legal advice and must not be signed or used as a real contract.

## PARTIES AND DATE

This Residential Rental Agreement ("Agreement") is made on 1 October 2026 between Meera Kulkarni, residing at 18 Gulmohar Enclave, Pune, Maharashtra 411038 ("Landlord"), and Aarav Deshmukh, residing at 42 Lakeview Road, Pune, Maharashtra 411007 ("Tenant"). The Landlord and Tenant are together referred to as the "Parties." All names and addresses in this document are fictional and created for software testing.

## 1. PREMISES

The Landlord lets to the Tenant the residential apartment identified as Flat 302, Building B, Cedar Residency, Survey Road, Pune, Maharashtra 411045, consisting of two bedrooms, one living room, one kitchen, two bathrooms, and one balcony ("Premises"). The Premises shall be used solely as the Tenant's private residence. The Tenant shall not use the Premises for a shop, clinic, warehouse, unlawful activity, or any activity that causes unreasonable disturbance to other residents.

## 2. TERM

The tenancy begins on 1 October 2026 and ends on 30 September 2027, a fixed term of twelve months, unless terminated earlier in accordance with this Agreement and applicable law. Possession will be handed over on the commencement date after the initial rent and security deposit have been received. Any extension must be recorded in a written document signed by both Parties; continued discussions alone do not create a renewal.

## 3. MONTHLY RENT

The Tenant shall pay monthly rent of n25,000 (Rupees Twenty-Five Thousand only), in advance, on or before the fifth calendar day of each month. Payment shall be made by bank transfer to the account designated in writing by the Landlord. The Tenant should retain transaction confirmations. If the fifth day falls on a bank holiday, payment may be made on the next banking day without a late fee.

## 4. SECURITY DEPOSIT

On signing, the Tenant shall pay an interest-free refundable security deposit of n75,000, equal to three months of the monthly rent. The deposit is security for unpaid rent, unpaid utility charges attributable to the tenancy, missing inventory items, and damage beyond reasonable wear and tear. The Landlord shall not treat the deposit as the final month's rent unless the Parties agree in writing.

## 5. DEPOSIT REFUND AND DEDUCTIONS

Within fifteen calendar days after the Tenant returns vacant possession and all keys, the Landlord shall refund the balance of the deposit. Any deduction must be itemised in writing and supported, where reasonably available, by photographs, invoices, or bills. No deduction shall be made for ordinary fading, minor scuffs from normal use, or ageing of fixtures. If a utility bill has not yet been issued, the Landlord may temporarily retain a reasonable estimated amount and settle the balance promptly after the bill arrives.

## 6. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS

The Landlord is responsible for structural repairs, concealed plumbing defects, major electrical faults not caused by the Tenant, and repair or replacement of supplied appliances that fail through ordinary use. The Tenant is responsible for routine cleanliness, replacing ordinary consumables such as light bulbs, and promptly reporting leaks, electrical hazards, pest activity, or damage. The Tenant shall not commission major repairs and deduct their cost from rent without written approval, except where emergency action is reasonably necessary to prevent immediate serious damage.

## 7. UTILITIES AND SOCIETY CHARGES

The Tenant shall pay electricity, piped gas, internet, and water charges measured or billed for the period of occupation. The Landlord shall pay property tax and any owner-only assessment. Monthly building maintenance of n2,000 shall be paid by the Landlord. The Tenant shall follow reasonable written rules of the residential society concerning waste disposal, parking, noise, and use of common areas.

## 8. NOTICE AND EARLY TERMINATION

Either Party may terminate this Agreement by giving sixty days' written notice. The Tenant may leave before the fixed term ends by giving the same notice and paying rent through the notice period, unless the Landlord agrees in writing to an earlier release. The Landlord may seek termination for material breach, non-payment, serious nuisance, or unlawful use, subject to applicable law and any required legal process. A notice may be delivered by email to the addresses confirmed by the Parties, with a copy by registered post or acknowledged message.

## 9. RENT ESCALATION

If the Parties renew the tenancy for a further term, the monthly rent will increase by five percent from the first day of the renewed term, unless a different amount is agreed in writing. No escalation applies during the initial twelve-month term.

## 10. INSPECTION AND PRIVACY

The Landlord may inspect the Premises after giving at least twenty-four hours' notice and arranging a mutually reasonable time, except in an emergency involving immediate risk to persons or property. The Landlord shall not enter merely for convenience or disclose the Tenant's personal information except as reasonably required to administer the tenancy or comply with law.

## 11. ALTERATIONS, SUBLETTING AND OCCUPANTS

The Tenant shall not make structural alterations, change locks without providing a working emergency key, assign the Agreement, or sublet the Premises without the Landlord's prior written consent. Ordinary removable furniture and non-damaging decorations are permitted. The approved occupants are Aarav Deshmukh and one dependent family member. The Tenant shall notify the Landlord of any material change in occupancy.

## 12. INVENTORY AND CONDITION

At handover, the Parties shall sign an inventory noting two ceiling fans, three air-conditioning units, a refrigerator, a washing machine, kitchen cabinets, bathroom fittings, and two sets of keys. The Parties should attach dated photographs of existing marks or defects. The Tenant shall return supplied items in substantially the same condition, allowing for reasonable wear and tear.

## 13. DEFAULT AND CURE

If either Party materially breaches this Agreement, the other Party shall give written notice describing the breach and a reasonable opportunity to cure it where cure is possible. Failure to pay rent within ten days after written demand is a material breach. Nothing in this clause authorises unlawful eviction, disconnection of essential services, or seizure of the Tenant's belongings.

## 14. DISPUTES AND GOVERNING LAW

The Parties shall first attempt to resolve disputes through a written discussion. If unresolved, disputes may be brought before a court or competent authority having jurisdiction over the Premises, subject to applicable law. This Agreement is governed by the laws applicable in India and the relevant state rules.

## 15. ENTIRE AGREEMENT AND SIGNATURES

This document and any signed inventory constitute the entire agreement between the Parties concerning the Premises and replace prior informal discussions on the same subject. Amendments must be written and signed by both Parties. Each Party confirms that they have had an opportunity to read and understand the terms.
